OCR-assisted Data Entry

A project-based automation workflow for extracting information from vehicle-inspection documents and entering data into a website.

WORKFLOW SUMMARY · CONCEPTUAL
Document image→Image processing / OCR→Extracted data→Web entry

Problem & objective

Reduce repetitive manual entry from paper vehicle-inspection records into the Da Nang Port website.

Contribution & solution

Used image processing and OCR as part of a workflow that extracted document information and automated website data entry.

Evidence & limitations

Past project-based freelance work. Document samples, field-level accuracy and time-saved measurements are not published as verified evidence here.

Evaluation plan for a reproducible demo

Measure field-level exact match, correction rate and failure handling on labelled documents. No accuracy or time-saving figure is claimed because verified measurements are unavailable.
