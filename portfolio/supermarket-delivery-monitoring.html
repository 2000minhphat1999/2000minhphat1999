Supermarket Delivery Monitoring

A project-based freelance Computer Vision workflow using video to detect and track activity related to supermarket deliveries.

WORKFLOW SUMMARY · CONCEPTUAL
Video frames→YOLOv5 detection→DeepSORT tracking→Track results

Problem & objective

Support delivery monitoring by identifying and following relevant objects through video.

Contribution & solution

Implemented a Computer Vision workflow using YOLOv5 for object detection and DeepSORT for object tracking.

Evidence & limitations

Past freelance project, delivered in a project-based engagement. This portfolio does not claim a verified accuracy, throughput or time-saving metric.

Evaluation plan for a reproducible demo

Report detector precision/recall and representative failure cases; for tracking, document identity switches and occlusion behavior. These are recommended measurements, not claimed results.
