02 / Engineering approach

Reliable software starts
with the workflow.

My working approach is to understand the process before optimizing the implementation. That means making rules explicit, checking edge cases and keeping the difference between verified behavior and assumptions visible.

01 / DISCOVERUnderstand the processClarify the operational goal, users and expected flow.
02 / MODELMake rules explicitMap data, state transitions and decision points.
03 / IMPLEMENTBuild & debugTranslate requirements into small, testable behaviors.
04 / VERIFYTest what mattersCheck expected behavior and communicate limitations.
